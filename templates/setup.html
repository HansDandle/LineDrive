TV guide

With a US ZIP or Canadian postal code you get a week of Gracenote's over-the-air listings, including stations from across the border if your area gets them. If your antenna reaches a city that isn't in your lineup, add that city's code too, separated by a comma (e.g. L2E 6S4, 14201); Test shows which of your channels still have no listings. The guide also uses SiliconDust's listings for your tuner, which add artwork and better rerun detection. Elsewhere, leave this empty: SiliconDust's guide on its own covers about a day ahead.

Recordings

Sizes are for full-HD (1080) stations; 720p stations like ABC and FOX come out smaller. 720p looks close to Standard on most TVs at a third of the size, and uses much less CPU, which helps when several shows record at once. Original keeps the broadcast untouched.

A graphics card takes the encoding off the CPU, so several shows can record at once on a small PC. Sizes come out close to the ones above, not exactly. If the card doesn't work here, recordings use the CPU and the home page says so. In Docker the card has to be passed into the container; see the README.

Extra files beside each recording

Jellyfin, Emby and Kodi read the .nfo for the plot and air date; without it they go by the file name. Plex ignores .nfo files.

Commercials {% if comskip_available %}

Comskip runs after each recording; it takes a few minutes per show and uses the CPU, and the recording appears in Jellyfin once it's done. Chapters: in Jellyfin 10.10+, the Chapter Segments plugin makes the “Commercial” chapters skippable; Kodi skips them from the .edl; Plex and Emby show the chapters to jump past. Cut: Comskip sometimes takes a scene for an ad, and a cut can't be put back, so the uncut original is kept in the recordings' .deleted folder for 7 days (move it back to restore it). Cuts are made on keyframes, so a second of ad may stay in, and a recording where Comskip's answer looks wrong gets chapters instead.

{% else %}
